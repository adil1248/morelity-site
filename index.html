Company acquired

Morelity

Morelity was acquired by Gooi Global / Gooi Mortgage. This page is kept online as a record of the company for anyone looking for it.

From 2015 to 2017, Morelity was an early mover in applying machine learning and audience modeling to help small and mid-size banks make better use of their data.
